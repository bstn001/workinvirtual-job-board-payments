# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions rather than people, protect private information, and respect different levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and abusive behavior are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@workinvirtual.com**. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
